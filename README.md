## Hi, I'm Anubhava Singla

I'm passionate about using data to inform public policy, which has been the focus of the projects I have contributed to over the past few years. I completed a Master of Development Practice at UC Berkeley's Goldman School of Public Policy (2024–2026).

### Data visualisation

- **[Is climate change causing a dengue crisis in Peru?](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/183a173b2b65493b9256437ad6455db0)**: an ArcGIS StoryMap on climate and health.

### Code

- **[Improving India's National Household Income Survey](https://github.com/anubhavasingla/NHIS_Capstone_Reproducible_Code)**: capstone for the World Bank's Global Poverty and Inequality Data Team. A synthetic-data evaluation of how the NHIS 2026 self-employment module measures annual income. (R)
- **[Infant mortality and household wealth in Nigeria](https://github.com/anubhavasingla/r-coding-sample)**: DHS birth histories linked to cluster GPS and WorldClim temperature data. (R)
- **[Group therapy for depression in Ghana](https://github.com/anubhavasingla/stata-coding-sample)**: evaluation of a randomised controlled trial, with treatment effects and gender heterogeneity. (Stata)
